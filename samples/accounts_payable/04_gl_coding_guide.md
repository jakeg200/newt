# GL Coding Guide

Synthetic Co — Finance. Use this guide to choose the general ledger (GL) code for each invoice line.

## Codes

| Code | Use for |
| --- | --- |
| 6100 | Software licences and subscriptions |
| 6200 | Professional services: legal, consulting, audit |
| 6300 | Travel and accommodation |
| 6400 | Office supplies and small equipment under £500 |
| 1600 | Equipment of £500 or more (capital; needs the asset register updated) |

If an invoice line could fit two codes, use the code on the PO line. If the PO has no code, ask the budget holder rather than guessing.

## Terms

PO means a purchase order raised and approved in the Approvals Portal before goods or services are ordered.

GRN means a goods received note, recorded in Xero when the goods or services arrive.

Budget holder refers to the person accountable for a cost centre's spending, named on the cost centre list.

Credit note means a document from the supplier that reduces or cancels an amount we were invoiced.
