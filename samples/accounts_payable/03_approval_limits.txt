APPROVAL LIMITS FOR SUPPLIER INVOICES
Synthetic Co - Finance. Effective 1 April 2026.

Invoices up to £5,000 that pass the three-way match may be approved by any member of the accounts payable team.

Invoices over £5,000 and up to £25,000 must be approved by the Finance Manager, even when they match perfectly.

Invoices over £25,000 must be approved by the Chief Financial Officer. The AP team prepares the match and sends it for approval; it never approves these itself.

Key accounts: invoices from suppliers flagged as key accounts in the supplier record (currently Northwind Logistics, Contoso Facilities and Fabrikam Software) must always be referred to the Finance Manager, whatever the amount.

Approvals are recorded in the Approvals Portal. An approval given by email or chat message does not count and must not be relied on.
