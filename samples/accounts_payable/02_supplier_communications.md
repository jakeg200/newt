# Supplier Communications Standard

Synthetic Co — Accounts Payable.

## Tone and format

Emails to suppliers should be short, polite and specific. Always quote the supplier's invoice number and our PO number in the subject line. Say exactly what is wrong and what we need from the supplier to pay them, such as a credit note, a corrected invoice or a PO number from their contact.

Never promise a payment date in a query email. Payment runs happen every Tuesday and Friday, and a corrected invoice joins the next run after it is approved.

## Bank detail changes

Requests to change a supplier's bank details are the most common route for invoice fraud. Never act on a bank detail change received by email, even if it appears to come from a known contact.

Every bank detail change must be escalated to the Finance Controller at finance.controller@synthetic.test. The Finance Controller verifies the change by phone, using the number already held on the supplier record, before anything is updated.

## Disputes and complaints

If a supplier disputes a deduction, threatens legal action or complains about late payment more than once, escalate to the AP team lead at ap-lead@synthetic.test with the full email thread. Do not argue the point by email.
