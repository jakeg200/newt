# AP Month-End Procedure

Synthetic Co — Accounts Payable. Run on the last three working days of each month.

Month-end close steps

1. Export all unapproved invoices from Xero and check each one has an owner.
2. Chase every invoice older than 14 days that is waiting on a supplier query.
3. Post accruals for goods received but not yet invoiced, using the GRN report.
4. Reconcile the supplier statements for the top 20 suppliers by spend.
5. Send the list of disputed invoices to the AP team lead at ap-lead@synthetic.test.
6. Confirm the payment run totals with the Finance Manager before release.
7. Lock the period in Xero once the Finance Controller signs off.

Access: the AP team needs read and draft access to Xero, read access to the Approvals Portal, and no access to bank payment release, which only the Finance Manager holds.

Accrual means a cost recorded in the month it was incurred, before the supplier has invoiced it.
