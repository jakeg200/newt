# Invoice Matching Policy

Synthetic Co — Accounts Payable. Owner: Finance Controller. Version 4, March 2026.

## Purpose

This policy sets out how the accounts payable team checks supplier invoices against purchase orders before payment. Its aim is to pay suppliers correctly and on time while stopping duplicate, inflated or fraudulent payments.

## Three-way match

Every invoice must be matched to an approved purchase order (PO) and a goods received note (GRN) before it is approved for payment. An invoice matches when the supplier, the line items, the quantities and the unit prices agree across all three documents.

## Tolerances

A price variance is within tolerance when the invoice total differs from the PO total by no more than 2% or £50, whichever is lower. Invoices within tolerance may be approved without contacting the supplier. Record the variance reason as "price" in the ledger.

A quantity variance is never within tolerance. If the invoiced quantity differs from the quantity on the GRN, query the supplier and ask for a credit note or a corrected invoice. Do not approve part of an invoice.

## Missing or closed purchase orders

If an invoice has no PO number, or the PO is closed, do not approve it. Escalate it to the budget holder named on the cost centre and ask them to raise or reopen a PO. Record the variance reason as "missing_po".

## Duplicates

Before approving, always search the ledger for the same supplier, invoice number and amount in the last 12 months. A suspected duplicate must be rejected and the supplier told which earlier invoice it duplicates. Record the variance reason as "duplicate".
