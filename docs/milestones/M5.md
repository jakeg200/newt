## Built

- **Statistics** (`app/eval/stats.py`): one-sided Clopper–Pearson bounds, cases needed for a target
  α, and the τ search (the lowest threshold whose bound clears α; when none does, the closest).
- **Confidence** (`app/eval/calibration.py`): self-consistency averaged with a logistic-regression
  model of the mentor's decisions. That model is trained only on decisions made before the case it
  scores (forward chaining), and the blend is calibrated with isotonic regression. Its weights are
  shown as "what drives your decisions".
- **Graduation** (`app/graduation/engine.py`): evidence = the latest backtest plus every live,
  scored prediction. Bars: upper bound ≤ α, 150+ cases above τ including 50+ live, coverage ≥ 40%.
  The mentor signs shadow → supervised. The manager signs supervised → autonomous, after 50+
  supervised cases and no failed audit. Each signature freezes τ and the calibration and records a
  track record.
- **Live work** (`app/live.py`): new live items get sealed predictions (a job), revealed and scored
  once the mentor acts. Supervised: drafts wait for one-tap approve, edit or reject, and the
  untouched-approval rate is tracked. Autonomous: allowed actions run (simulated for now), and about
  1 in 10 handled cases becomes a blind audit.
- **Hand-backs** (`app/graduation/handback.py`): below τ, unlike anything seen, missing information,
  or a hard rule from the config (amount thresholds are parsed, so "amount above 5000 GBP" fires on
  £8,450).
- **Action policy** (`app/actions/policy.py`): actions are derived from the answer, never chosen by
  the agent. Whitelist per task type. Replies go only to the original sender: no links, no other
  addresses, no attachments or extra recipients. Fields must be named and valid. Blocked actions
  are logged with reasons. Live answers are validated against the task's output contract first.
- **Reviews**: blind audits (the card never carries the agent's answer). Demotion follows the
  brief's two rules. Drift checks compare how often recent items are unfamiliar against the
  graduation record. Re-probation on the frozen record runs after model swaps, rule changes and
  newly approved handbooks.
- **Track record** (`app/graduation/track_record.py`): JSON and a one-page PDF with the brief's
  fields.
- **Web**: Task board (stage and progress per task type; graduation proposal with checks, examples
  and criteria; sign buttons by role; drift check; model swap; PDF export) and Autonomy (drafts to
  approve, blind audits, hand-backs, digest).

## On the sample data

The sample history has the mentor go against their own pattern 5% of the time, so the offline
agent can't clear a 2% bound: the board shows 113 confident cases with 3 disagreements, a 6.7%
bound, and about 273 more clean cases needed. That's the bar doing its job. The tests graduate a
team with consistent history to exercise the full journey.

## Left for later

- Actions are simulated. Sending replies and updating fields through the customer's systems needs
  the production connectors (Gmail drafts/send scopes, the REST target's write API).
- Measure re-probation time with Claude. If it's over 10 minutes, cap the frozen record at 100
  cases or raise `BACKTEST_CONCURRENCY`.
- Calibration is fitted and τ chosen on the same evidence (as the brief describes). With more data,
  hold out a split for the bound.
- Bars (α, minimum cases, live minimum, coverage) come from the task config and module constants;
  a mentor-facing editor for them isn't built.
- Drift is checked on demand; schedule it weekly in the worker.
- A demo dataset that graduates (consistent history plus live traffic) for showing supervised and
  autonomous modes in the UI.
